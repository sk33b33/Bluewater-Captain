Bluewater Captain

Stage 6 prototype: earn licenses, pick a vessel and a contract on the mission board, plan your route across the bay on the chart, and sail it through the day's weather to the pier. All art is temporary placeholder.

Touch: slide the left lever up for throttle (it stays where you leave it), slide the bottom-right bar to steer, drag the water to look around. Steering needs some speed.
