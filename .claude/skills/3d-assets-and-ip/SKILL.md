# Skill: 3D Assets and Intellectual Property

- Prefer original assets or assets with clearly compatible licenses.
- Track asset name, creator/vendor, source, license, attribution, permitted platforms, and proof of permission.
- Do not assume that a publicly visible model or image is free to reuse.
- Famous vessels and franchise ships can involve trademark, copyright, design, or other rights. Flag legal review before commercial/public distribution.
- Use original stand-in designs until rights are cleared.
- Test scale, collision meshes, material performance, and level of detail in the actual engine.
- Never present temporary assets as final.
